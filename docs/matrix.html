Dongles

Wireless feeder dongles

Plug into a Gotek on another machine and present a disk that’s flung to them from a touchscreen. The current firmware is the Webby (HD) edition — the older classic DD-only SuperMini build is retired. The ESP32-S3-Zero runs the SuperMini Webby image; the XIAO also has an older HD “classic” build.

The ESP32-S3-Zero

Not a separate firmware — the Zero runs the SuperMini Webby image unchanged, so its column is “SuperMini · Zero.” Its one difference is the LED: a single WS2812 RGB on GPIO21 instead of two discrete LEDs.
